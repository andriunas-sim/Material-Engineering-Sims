Heat energy ≈ R × T, with R = 8.314 J/(mol·K) and T in kelvin.

This is one bond being torn apart, which is what happens to gases in a shock layer or an arc. Melting and boiling are different processes and need more than this picture. Bond energies are typical values; curve shapes are illustrative (Morse potential).
