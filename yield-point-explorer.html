Illustrative values for a mild steel: E = 210 GPa, ReH = 280 MPa, ReL ≈ 250 MPa, Lüders strain 2 %. The curve stops at 4 % strain, long before the tensile strength. Lattice shear, serration size and bar extension are exaggerated so they can be seen. A real grain is tens of thousands of atoms across and holds many dislocations; the lattice view shows one of them.
